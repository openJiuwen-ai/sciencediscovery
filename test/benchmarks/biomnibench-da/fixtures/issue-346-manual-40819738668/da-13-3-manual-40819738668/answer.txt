QUESTION: Which proteins correlate with changes in body composition (fat percentage and
breast volume) following feminizing GAHT?

SHORT ANSWER
Using the pre-computed per-protein mixed-linear-model results (5,278 Olink Explore HT
proteins; model: protein NPX ~ body-composition variable + age + baseline BMI +
random intercept per participant), and calling significance at Benjamini-Hochberg
FDR < 0.05:

  * PERCENT BODY FAT  -> 38 proteins (36 negative, 2 positive).
  * BREAST VOLUME     -> 249 proteins (237 negative, 12 positive).
  * SHARED (both)     -> 28 proteins, all 28 with the same sign for both traits
                         (27 down, leptin up); 63.6-fold more overlap than chance
                         (Fisher OR 63.6, p = 5.1e-30).
  * UNION             -> 259 distinct proteins.

The percent-fat signature is leptin plus a tightly co-ordinated set of keratinocyte /
stratified-epithelial, mucosal, matrix and inflammatory proteins. The breast-volume
signature is much broader, is ~80% negative genome-wide, and is anchored by INSL3
(falling) and prolactin (rising) - i.e. it behaves largely as a readout of cumulative
GAHT exposure / androgen suppression rather than of breast tissue per se.

5. INTERPRETATION

(a) Internal positive control passes. Leptin is the 2nd-strongest fat association in
the entire panel (+0.196 NPX per +1 %fat, padj 5.5e-04) and rises with fat exactly as
established physiology predicts (Zhang 1994 Nature; Considine 1996 NEJM). It is,
however, the ONLY classical adipocyte protein that is significant: FABP4, CFD/adipsin,
RBP4, NAMPT, LEPR, LPL and PLIN1 are all null (padj >= 0.15), and adiponectin (ADIPOQ)
is not on the panel. The fat signature is therefore not a generic adipokine response.

(b) Percent fat tracks a coordinated decline of stratified-epithelial / barrier
proteins. Eight of the eleven keratinocyte-differentiation genes measured are fat hits,
all negative, together with mucosal (CA6, PLB1, PGLYRP3, MUC1, CBLIF) and matrix
(MMP3, SOST, COL15A1) proteins. Two non-exclusive readings fit: a genuine
adiposity-epidermal axis (adiposity perturbs epidermal differentiation/barrier
function), or a shared hormonal driver, since androgens are major trophic regulators of
sebaceous/keratinocyte biology and androgen suppression and fat gain occur together
during feminizing GAHT. These data cannot separate the two.

(c) Breast volume behaves largely as a treatment-exposure readout. The single
strongest breast association in the panel is INSL3 (-0.0152, padj 3.3e-05), a
Leydig-cell-specific marker of gonadal androgen output (Ivell & Anand-Ivell 2009),
with a null fat association; the second strongest is prolactin (+0.00435,
padj 2.8e-04), whose secretion is stimulated by oestrogen (Freeman 2000). A
falling androgen marker and a rising oestrogen-responsive hormone both tracking breast
volume is the signature of cumulative GAHT exposure, which is also what drives breast
growth. In addition, 79.6% of ALL 5,278 proteins have a negative breast-volume slope
(binomial p < 1e-300) - a global downward shift consistent with oestrogen-driven
plasma-volume expansion / haemodilution (Stachenfeld 2008). Breast-volume hits should
therefore be read as partly a treatment-exposure/dilution signature.

(d) The 28 shared proteins are a coherent epithelial-matrix-inflammatory module, but
their sharing is not evidence of a common body-composition mechanism. Because both fat
percentage and breast volume increase during GAHT and are therefore collinear with time
on treatment and cumulative hormone exposure, the shared set is most parsimoniously a
GAHT-exposure signature that both traits inherit. The 10 fat-specific proteins listed in
section 3 are the better candidates for fat biology proper.

(e) Other mechanistically informative hits: SOST/sclerostin (down with both) is the
osteocyte Wnt antagonist and bone-turnover readout (Bonewald 2011), consistent with the
known inverse adiposity-sclerostin relationship and with GAHT's bone-active hormonal
changes; MMP3 and COL15A1 indicate matrix remodelling; PSG1, CFC1, ENDOU and PAEP are
placental/pregnancy-associated, which matters because the cohort includes a pregnancy
comparator arm and suggests those contrasts contribute to the fitted slopes.


6. WHAT THIS ANALYSIS CANNOT CONCLUDE
  * Only pre-computed results were available (no NPX matrix, sample metadata, arm
    labels, confidence intervals, standard errors or raw p-values); nothing was
    re-fitted and the model specification could not be verified. The supplied
    adj.p.value columns were confirmed to be already FDR-adjusted (re-running BH on
    them yields 0 hits, versus 38 and 249 as supplied), so they were used as-is.
  * Percent fat and breast volume were modelled in separate single-predictor models, so
    the 28 shared proteins are NOT shown to be independently associated with both
    traits; the two traits are correlated and both move with treatment.
  * Causal direction is not identifiable: "protein per unit fat" is equally compatible
    with adipose tissue driving the protein, the protein driving adiposity, or a third
    variable (oestradiol dose, time on treatment, plasma volume) driving both.
  * A random-intercept model with a time-varying predictor returns a variance-weighted
    blend of within-person and between-person slopes; with two time points these are
    not separable, so "correlates with change" is an interpretation rather than a
    demonstrated within-person effect.
  * The two estimate columns are on different units (sd ratio 32.8; the breast-volume
    unit is unstated), so cross-trait magnitudes are not comparable - only signs,
    ranks and within-trait ratios are used here.
  * The functional annotation is exploratory (hand-curated gene sets, chosen after
    inspecting the hit list, uncorrected across seven categories, panel-based
    background): hypotheses, not confirmatory enrichment.
  * Coverage gaps: adiponectin, IGF-1, IGF-2 and CRP are absent from the panel, so no
    claim of "no association" can be made for them.
  * Generalisation is limited to a 6-month feminizing-GAHT cohort; no arm-stratified
    (CPA vs spironolactone) analysis was possible with the supplied table.
